Will Blygger stay decentralized as it grows?

Blygger leaves discovery and ranking to services built on top, so the attention loop it removes could come back from outside the protocol. blyg-watch will measure how concentrated the links between blygs are, and how that changes over time.

  • Quote copies a passage from another blyg. Most of the conversation, and never read as approval.
  • Stub a new item declaring itself a response to one item elsewhere. The protocol has no reply.
  • Fork a new item started from a pinned version of someone else's.

Why ask

Open protocols have concentrated before. "People don't want to run their own servers, and never will," Moxie Marlinspike wrote in 2022, and in growing networks new links go to sites that are already well linked (Barabási and Albert, 1999). Each case below is one of the indicators.

Mastodon: "10% of instances host almost half of the users" (Raman et al., 2019)

What the crawl cannot see

The protocol forgets part of the answer by design. The crawl sees every blyg on the public list, every item in each archive index, and the links in each item's latest and pinned versions. It does not see these five.

Not visibleWhy
Older versions of an item, unless pinned
No route serves an unpinned older version. §8.4
Quotes and stubs in an item later withdrawn
Withdrawal drops stub_of, generated and transclusions. §9
Who links to a blyg
Mentions go to each blyg's inbox, not to its public files.
Who follows whom
Subscriptions are private, and there is no follower graph. §16.8
Blygs not on the public list
The crawl starts from blygger.com's blygs.opml, which lists 25 blygs.

Snapshots on a schedule recover part of what a single later crawl would miss. That is step 4.

Our rule: detect, never feed

Blygger refuses counts on purpose. A machine-readable list of who responded is "the first surface a count could attach to", and a reader may order what it reads provided "nothing derived is published". §16.6d

In his talk on Blygger, Venkatesh Rao says mentions are listed, never counted, and that a client which rolls them up privately is its own business.

So clients may count privately; blyg-watch watches for published counts and publishes none. Every indicator tracks a harm the spec itself names, and no indicator ranks a person or a blyg.

With 25 blygs even an unnamed concentration figure points at someone, so the last indicator is shown as a trend only until the network is larger. The list was offered to the protocol's authors on 2026-10-29. The indicators are blyg-watch's own choice, kept independent of the spec, so the watch does not shape what it watches.
