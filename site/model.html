Model

Facets models of a blyg and of the network. They name the flows the measurement counts and show where history sits.

Not yet Nothing here is built yet.

What this step will hold

A model of one blyg
Its public files, its items and their versions, and the links that leave it.
A model of the network
Blygs as separate origins, and quotes, stubs and forks as the flows between them.
Where it will live
The model/ folder in the repository.

What is not claimed

Model runs are conditional: if this structure and these numbers, then this. A run is not a measurement of the network.
