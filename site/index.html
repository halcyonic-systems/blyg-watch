The first question

Will Blygger stay decentralized as it grows?

Blygger refuses replies, counts, a follower graph and ranking. It leaves discovery and ranking to services built on top, so the attention loop it removes can come back from outside the protocol. The protocol forgets part of the answer by design.

Your job

Check what can be seen, and what can't.

What the crawl cannot see

It sees every blyg on the public list, every item in each archive index, and the links in each item's latest version and its pinned versions. Not these:

Not visibleWhy
Older versions of an item, unless pinnedNo route serves an unpinned older version §8.4
Quotes and stubs in an item later withdrawnWithdrawal drops stub_of, generated and transclusions §9
Who links to a blygMentions go to each blyg's inbox, not to its public files
Who follows whomSubscriptions are private; there is no follower graph §16.8
Blygs not on the public listThe crawl starts from blygger.com's blygs.opml

Snapshots on a schedule recover part of what a single later crawl would miss.

Measuring without bringing back counts

Blygger refuses counts on purpose: a machine-readable list of who responded is "the first surface a count could attach to," and a reader may order what it reads provided "nothing derived is published" §16.6d. So this tool detects that loop and never feeds it. Every indicator tracks a warning the spec itself names.

The spec's warningWhereIndicator
"Anything that can serve files can serve a blyg"§1, §4How many blygs depend on one client or one host
The generator string is "the only census of the ecosystem that exists without anyone registering anything"§3.2Client diversity over time
"every unpinned remote quote is a veto held by a stranger"§10.2Share of cross-blyg quotes that point at unpinned versions
Stubs without a response fill "every target's response signal with non-responses"§10.6Share of stubs with no editorial content
An aggregator "never re-emits" and never stubs for its members§13.5Items that re-emit others' content
The blogroll "claims no completeness"§11Blygs reachable only through the public list, against blygs reachable through blogrolls
Attention concentrating on a few origins§16.8One network-wide measure of how skewed links are, with no names

No indicator ranks a person or a blyg. With 25 blygs even an unnamed concentration figure points at someone, so the last row is shown as a trend only until the network is larger. The indicator list is offered to the protocol's authors for comment before the first full crawl.

Builds on

The Blygger conformance toolkit by Aneesh Sathe already crawls the directory and draws a network report. Its question is conformance; this one asks about concentration and history over time, and reuses that work where it can.

What is not claimed

The Lean models spec v0.4 as written, a pre-1.0 draft, not any client or whether a publisher keeps its promises. Model runs are conditional. Measurements cover the blygs on the public list at each snapshot. Nothing is proven until lake build checks it.
