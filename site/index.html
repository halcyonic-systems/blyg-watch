Watching the Blygger network as a system.

Blygger is a way to publish with no replies, counts, follower graph or ranking. blyg-watch reads the network's public files over time, to see whether it stays spread out as it grows.

Start with the question Source on GitHub

Early Nothing is measured or proven yet.

The question

Will Blygger stay decentralized as it grows?

Blygger leaves discovery and ranking to services built on top, so the attention loop it removes could come back from outside. Part of the answer the protocol forgets by design.

Your job

Check what can be seen, and what can't.

Detect, never feed No published counts, no ranking of any person or blyg.
