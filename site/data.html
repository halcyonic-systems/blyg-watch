Data

Dated snapshots of the public list of blygs, and the indicators measured from them. The dashboard grows out of this step.

What this step will hold

Snapshots
Dated reads of every blyg on blygger.com's blygs.opml, which lists 25 today. They are collected by a reader that subscribes to every blyg, once a week, one request at a time, and publishes nothing into the network. Its first run reads every item once, for the history a feed does not carry.
Indicators
The seven from step 1, each measured per snapshot.
Charts
Each indicator over time as a line; client and host shares as bars (software, not people); the network's link structure drawn as a graph without names, shown only if it cannot be read as a ranking (an open decision).
Where it lives
The reader is in crawl/, with its etiquette and what it keeps in crawl/README.md. Snapshots go in data/, one dated file a week, never edited. Charts will read them.

Builds on

The Blygger conformance toolkit by Aneesh Sathe already crawls the directory and draws a network report. Its question is whether blygs follow the spec. This step asks about concentration and history over time, and reuses that work where it can.

What is not claimed

  • Measurements cover the blygs on the public list at each snapshot, not every blyg that exists.
  • No figure names or ranks a person or a blyg.
  • Snapshots keep links, never the text of anyone's items.
