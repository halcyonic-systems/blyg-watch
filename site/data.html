What this step will hold

Snapshots
Crawls of every blyg on blygger.com's blygs.opml, which lists 25 today, on a schedule and dated.
Indicators
The seven from step 1, each measured per snapshot.
Where it will live
The crawl/ and data/ folders. Charts read data/*.json.

Builds on

The Blygger conformance toolkit by Aneesh Sathe already crawls the directory and draws a network report. Its question is whether blygs follow the spec. This step asks about concentration and history over time, and reuses that work where it can.

What is not claimed

  • Measurements cover the blygs on the public list at each snapshot, not every blyg that exists.
  • No figure names or ranks a person or a blyg.
